Free tools for EVE Online

New Eden Tools

Find the nearest market orders, plan capital jumps, and plan stargate routes with the same settings as the in-game autopilot. They run in your browser and on your phone, and you can install them on Windows. No login.

Market Finder

Where can I buy or sell this?

Search any item and see every buy and sell order across New Eden, sorted by price and by jumps from where you are.

  • Live orders from every region
  • Jump count from your system, shortest or high-sec only
  • Quickbar for items you check often

Jump Planner

Capital jumps, fuel & sov map

Plan capital jumps across New Eden and see how much fuel you'll need, on a live sovereignty map.

  • Every jump-capable hull, from carriers and dreads to JFs, Black Ops and Rorquals
  • Fuel for each jump and the whole trip, with your Jump Fuel Conservation level, m³ and ISK
  • Fatigue and timer estimates, plus cyno rules

Gate Planner

Stargate routes, autopilot style

Plan gate routes with the in-game route settings: Shorter, Safer, or Less Secure, plus the security penalty slider.

  • Avoid pod-kill hotspots, Triglavian & EDENCOM systems
  • Your jump bridges, waypoints and avoidance list
  • Live kills for the last hour along the route

📱 Put them on your phone

Open a tool, then add it to your home screen. It opens full screen like a normal app and remembers your settings.

  1. iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android (Chrome): tap ⋮, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

On the maps, drag to pan, pinch to zoom, and tap a system for details. Swipe up the panel at the bottom for the planner.

🖥️ Windows desktop apps

Jump Planner and Gate Planner can also run as Windows apps with their own desktop icons. Download the code from GitHub, open an app's folder (e.g. apps/eve-jump-planner), and run:

npm install then npm run dist

That builds an installer that puts the icon on your desktop. Each app's README has the details.
